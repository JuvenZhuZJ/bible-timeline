    About this timeline

    This timeline shows the people of the Bible from Adam to the first-century Christians. Each dot is a person, and each large node is a turning point in Bible history.

    Moving around

    Drag the timeline to move through time, and scroll (or pinch on a phone) to zoom in and out. More people appear as you zoom in. The + and − buttons zoom too, and the button under them brings back the whole timeline, as does clicking the logo. The bar along the bottom shows all of history at once: click or drag on it to jump to a period. To find someone, type their name in the search box. The language button in the top bar switches between English and Chinese.

    Looking at a person

    Hover over a dot (or tap it) to see the person’s portrait, lifespan and family links. Click it (or tap again) to open their window. In a window you can click a relative to open theirs, and click a dot on the lifespan bar to read about something that happened in their life. Bible references open the verse in the New World Translation online. Key figures also list the other key figures who lived at the same time. Open several windows to compare lives side by side; their lifespans stay under the main line, and Fold all or Close all at the bottom tidies them up at once.

    Who is shown

    The people button in the top bar switches between everyone named in the Bible, the key figures, and only the line from Adam to Jesus (Luke 3:23-38; Matthew 1:1-16).

    Reading the dates

    Each birth and death year is one of three kinds, and the Legend button shows how each is drawn. A solid dot means the year is worked out from ages and reign lengths the Bible gives. A ring with a small dot means the year is estimated: the Bible gives a clue, such as an age at death, but no exact year. A dashed ring means the year is assumed: the Bible does not record it, so the person is placed by their generation or the period they appear in. Most people have assumed dates, so take their place on the timeline as a rough guide. “c.” before a year means it is approximate.

    Places

    When a window is open, a map shows the places the Bible connects with that person, numbered in the order of their life, with the roads and sea routes between them. Each activity that happened somewhere is tagged with its place, and each major event shows where it happened. On a phone the map sits at the top of the window. A dashed pin means the location is uncertain.

    Sources

    Bible references, quotations and name spellings follow the New World Translation of the Holy Scriptures (Study Edition). People and family relationships come from Insight on the Scriptures (Vols. 1 and 2); the summaries are written for this site. Years follow the chronology in Insight on the Scriptures and the charts in the New World Translation. Locations come from OpenBible.info, checked against Insight on the Scriptures and the New World Translation maps. Portraits are drawings for illustration only.
