    About this timeline

    Every dot is a person; every large node is a turning point in Bible history. Zoom in to reveal more people. Hover (or tap) a dot to see their portrait, lifespan and family links; click (or tap again) to open a window. Open several windows to compare lives side by side, and use Fold all or Close all at the bottom to tidy them up at once. Their lifespans stay pinned under the main line. In a window, tap a relative to open theirs.

    Who is shown

    The people button in the top bar switches between everyone named in the Bible, the key figures, and only the line from Adam to Jesus (Luke 3:23-38; Matthew 1:1-16).

    Bible text

    Scripture references, quotations and name spellings follow the New World Translation of the Holy Scriptures (Study Edition).

    People

    The list of people and their relationships comes from the person articles in Insight on the Scriptures (Vols. 1 & 2). Summaries are written for this site.

    Dates

    Years follow the chronology in Insight on the Scriptures (article “Chronology”), anchored on 607 B.C.E. and 539 B.C.E., with lifespans calculated from ages stated in the Bible. Most people have no recorded birth or death; their dates are assumed placeholders estimated from their generation or the period they appear in, drawn with dashed outlines and listed in data/ASSUMED_DATES.md for review.

    Places

    A map shows the places the Bible puts the person whose window is in use, numbered in the order of their life; each activity that happened somewhere has a place tag, and each major event shows where it happened. On a phone the map sits at the top of the person’s window. Locations come from the OpenBible.info Bible Geocoding Data, checked against Insight on the Scriptures and the maps in the New World Translation. A dashed pin means the site is uncertain.

    Portraits

    Portraits are generated line drawings for illustration only. You can replace any of them with an image by adding a portrait path in data/characters.js.
